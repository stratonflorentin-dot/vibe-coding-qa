# Product Research Report Template

Adapt sections to the user's question and available evidence. For a quick scan, keep the report short but include scope, at least the strongest available sources, major unknowns, and a validation recommendation. Use **Unknown/Not found** rather than filling gaps with guesses. Add inline source links immediately after factual claims and include this research's access date.

## 1. Executive decision

- **Recommendation:** Go / Revise / No-go / Preliminary only
- **Confidence:** High / Medium / Low, with reason
- **What is being built:** [one-sentence description]
- **For whom and where:** [user, buyer, geography]
- **Most important evidence:** [linked facts]
- **Biggest risk or unknown:** [why it matters]
- **Next validation action:** [test, metric, owner, decision rule]
